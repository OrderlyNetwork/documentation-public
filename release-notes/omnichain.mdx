        width: "calc(80% - 30px)",
      }}
    >

      - **Pending-order amendment protection** — Each pending order can be successfully amended up to 1,000 times. Further amendment requests are rejected without changing the existing order; cancel, fill, and settlement flows are unaffected
      - **More consistent collateral and withdrawal checks** — Improved alignment between risk-engine withdrawal validation and position-service free-collateral calculations
      - **Isolated-margin risk handling improvements** — Improved risk calculations for isolated-order amendments, pending-order cleanup, liquidation-lock checks, and isolated-margin operations
      - **Risk-engine performance improvements** — Enhanced selected risk-engine processing paths and runtime handling to improve efficiency and resilience under load
    </div>

  </div>
