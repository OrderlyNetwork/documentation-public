### COLLECTION OF INFORMATION

**Information You Provide to Us**

The categories of personal information we collect depend on how you interact with Orderly and its Services. Orderly collects information that you provide us, information we obtain automatically when you use our Services or participate in our marketing campaign, and information from third-party sources and other organizations. The types of personal information we may collect from you include, but not limited to, name, cryptocurrency wallet address and social media information (e.g: Twitter handle, Discord name), and any other information you choose to provide.

**Builder Surveys and Product Feedback**

When you participate in a builder survey or provide product feedback, we may collect your survey responses, free-text comments, broker or DEX identifiers, account or wallet identifiers, locale, submission timestamps, outreach preferences, and optional social media or Telegram contact information.

Survey responses may be associated with your Orderly builder account and are not anonymous. We use this information to understand how builders discover and use Orderly, improve our products and services, provide support, evaluate potential collaboration opportunities, and communicate with you where you have requested such communication.

Providing survey responses and contact information is optional. Please do not include private keys, credentials, confidential business information, sensitive personal information, or information relating to another person in a free-text response.

Where you choose to provide a Telegram handle or other contact information and agree to outreach, Orderly may contact you for product feedback, product-improvement discussions, support, or potential collaboration. You may withdraw this permission at any time by contacting Orderly support through [Discord](https://discord.gg/OrderlyNetwork) or [Telegram](https://t.me/Orderly_Discussions). Do not post personal information in a public channel; continue your request in a private support conversation.

**Information We Collect Automatically When You Interact with Us**

Orderly may collect certain information automatically when you use our Services. Examples of this information include but not limited to, cookie identifiers, internet protocol (IP) address, trading volume, mobile carrier, MAC address, user settings, mobile ad identifiers, Internet service provider, browser or device information, location information and metadata about information you provide to Orderly. We may also automatically collect information pertaining to your use of our Services. Examples of this information may be related to your pages visited, links clicked, content interacted with, duration and frequency of your activities and other information about your use of our Services.

**Information We Collect from Other Sources**

We obtain information from third-party sources. For example, we may collect information about you from identity verification services, data analytics providers, mailing list providers (if applicable) and public blockchains. Additionally, our Services may allow you to provide information about your family or friends through our referral service, which allows you to forward or share certain information with Orderly. Please share information only for people that you know.

**Public Blockchains**

We obtain information from public blockchains. Such information includes but is not limited to on-chain account information and transaction information. This information is used to improve Orderly Network and our services and products.

**Data Retention**

We store other personal data for as long as necessary to carry out the purposes for which we originally collected it and for other legitimate business purposes, including to meet our legal, regulatory, or other compliance obligations.

Identifiable builder survey responses and related free-text comments are retained for up to 12 months after submission, after which they are deleted or irreversibly aggregated or de-identified, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or compliance purposes.

Optional Telegram handles and other outreach contact information are retained until you withdraw your consent or for up to 12 months after the last relevant interaction, whichever occurs first, unless continued retention is legally required.

We may retain a limited record that a survey was completed, deferred, or skipped for longer where necessary to manage survey eligibility and prevent repeated requests. This limited record will not contain the survey answers or contact information.

Deleted information may remain in encrypted backups until those backups expire under our normal backup-retention schedule.

**Age Requirement**

We do not target our websites, mobile apps, API, or any of the Services for children. We do not intentionally collect information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently received personal data from a user under the age of 18 (or any lower age pursuant to applicable law), we will delete such information from our records.
