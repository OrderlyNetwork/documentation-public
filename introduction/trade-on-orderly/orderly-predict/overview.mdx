Orderly Predict is a market on BTC, settled every 5 minutes, where you trade on whether the price finishes a round up or down. You can place a trade well before a round's 5-minute decision window starts -- see [Rounds and Settlement](/introduction/trade-on-orderly/orderly-predict/rounds-and-settlement) for when a round opens. This page explains how UP and DOWN trades work, how their prices relate to each other, and how Orderly Predict differs from perpetual futures.

## How UP and DOWN prices work

When you trade on Orderly Predict, you're paying a cost per share for a payout that depends on the round's outcome, not buying a direct read on probability.

- If UP is priced at \$0.57, you pay \$0.57 per share. If BTC finishes the round higher than the round's strike, each share pays \$1 -- a \$0.43 gain before trading fees.
- If your side loses, the share pays out \$0, and the \$0.57 you paid is gone.
- If the round ties, every share -- UP and DOWN alike -- pays out \$0.50.
- Because ties settle at \$0.50, a price is not the same as the probability that BTC finishes higher.

A trading fee applies when your order fills; see [Orderly Predict fees](/introduction/trade-on-orderly/trading-basics/trading-fees#orderly-predict-fees). There's no separate fee when a round settles.

## UP and DOWN are two sides of one trade

Every round has exactly one outcome: UP, DOWN, or a tie. They aren't two separate markets -- they're two views of the same trade.

- For any single fill, the DOWN price is \$1 minus the UP price. The two prices you see on screen can add up to slightly more than \$1; see [Prices shown are prices you can trade at](#prices-shown-are-prices-you-can-trade-at).
- Your position shows as UP or DOWN, depending on which side you bought.
- Because the two sides mirror each other, there's a single order book for UP, and the DOWN price is derived from it.

## Prices shown are prices you can trade at

The UP and DOWN prices you see are executable prices, not a mid-price:

- The UP price shown is the best price you could buy UP at right now.
- The DOWN price shown is the best price you could buy DOWN at right now.

Because both sides are quoted at the price you'd actually trade at, UP + DOWN usually add up to slightly more than \$1 -- the difference is the market's spread, not an error. In thin markets, the gap can be wider.

## No leverage, no liquidation, no funding

Orderly Predict works differently from perpetual futures:

- **No leverage.** Every position is fully collateralized; there's nothing to borrow.
- **No liquidation.** Because positions are fully collateralized up front, there's no maintenance margin to fall below and nothing to liquidate.
- **No funding.** Rounds settle within minutes, so there's no funding rate.

## Availability

Whether Orderly Predict is available depends on the app you trade on -- Builders choose whether to enable it for their users. Check the terms of the app you're trading on before you trade, or see [Builders](/introduction/trade-on-orderly/builders) for the current list. Orderly Predict is also subject to its own [Supplemental Terms](/introduction/trade-on-orderly/orderly-predict/supplemental-terms) and is not offered to US Persons or Restricted Persons.
